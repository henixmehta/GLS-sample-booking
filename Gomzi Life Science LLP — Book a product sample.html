Sample order terms

  1. Samples are for evaluation of taste, texture and quality only and may not be resold.
  2. Sample prices include GST. Shipping within India is free.
  3. Orders are dispatched within 2–4 working days of payment. Delivery time depends on your pincode and courier.
  4. Packaging and labels on samples are generic. Your logo, label design and final pack are made only after a bulk order is confirmed.
  5. Bulk production may vary slightly in colour and taste from the sample because of natural variation in raw materials.
  6. Sample payments are non-refundable once dispatched. If a pack arrives damaged, share a photo within 48 hours of delivery and we’ll replace it.
  7. Nutrition values shown are approximate. Final label values are confirmed by lab testing for your batch.
  8. Bulk orders start from 50–100 kg per product and are quoted separately.
  9. Your contact and address details are used only to process this order and contact you about it.
